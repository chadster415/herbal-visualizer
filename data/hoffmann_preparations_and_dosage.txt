Common: Black Mustard
Preparations and Dosage: Mustard is most commonly used as a poultice, which can be made by mixing 100 g (4 oz) of freshly ground mustard seeds with enough warm water (at about 45°C or 110°F) to form a thick paste. Spread this mixture onto a piece of cloth the size of the body area to be covered. To prevent the paste from sticking to the skin, first lay a piece of dampened gauze on the skin. Apply the cloth spread with mustard and remove after 1 minute. The skin may be reddened by this treatment, but may be soothed by the application of olive oil afterward. To make an infusion, pour 1 cup of boiling water over 1 teaspoon of mustard flour (powder) and infuse for 5 minutes. This may be drunk three times a day. For a footbath, make an infusion using 1 tablespoon of bruised seeds to 1 liter (2 pints) of boiling water.
